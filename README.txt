SPELL THE STACK - བརྩེགས་ཡིག  (all-stacks version)
=================================================
Upload this whole folder. Open index.html.

1 - LEARN: choose a stack (ཡ་བཏགས ར་བཏགས ལ་བཏགས ར་མགོ ལ་མགོ ས་མགོ). Syllables are listed
    from easy to harder (Level 1 to 5):
      1 stack + vowel          2 + simple suffix (ག ང བ མ འ པ)
      3 + suffix that changes the sound (ད ན ར ལ ས)
      4 prefix + stack         5 post-suffix (ད ས)
    Tap a syllable, then "Build it slowly": letters are added one by one (root first, then top/bottom
    letter, vowel, prefix, suffix, post-suffix). Earlier letters stay on screen and turn green,
    each new letter plays its sound, the root+vowel sound plays, then root+suffix (the sound
    changes), and last the whole syllable. Speed: Slow / Normal.
    BUILD IT YOURSELF: tap the letter boxes (or the button) and the child builds the syllable
    by tapping - each tap adds that letter to the big word and plays its sound; tap again to remove.
    ANIMATION: each letter flies from its box up into the big word, which pops and glows when
    the syllable is complete (turned off automatically if the device asks for reduced motion).
    NEXT / BACK buttons move through the list; Next starts a fresh self-build.
    REWARDS: every syllable built gives a star (saved in the browser), confetti and a chime,
    a green tick on the syllable, and titles as stars grow (Sprout, Climber, Stack Star,
    Stack Master, Champion, Dragon Legend). Titles are in the TT list in index.html.
2 - SPELL GAME: pick a stack and a level.

AUDIO (you record; mp3)
  audio/RECORDING-LIST.csv lists every file name, in the order to record
  (single letters first, then Level 1 ... Level 5, most common first).
  audio/letter/  each single letter and vowel sign alone (about 30 files - record these first)
  audio/unit/    root letter(s) + vowel, before any suffix
  audio/syl/     whole syllables (and root+suffix before a post-suffix)
  Missing audio does not break anything: the step is shown and says "audio not recorded yet".

WORDS
  words.js holds every stacked syllable (built from an open Tibetan word list, so it is
  Tibetan spelling, not checked against the DDC dictionary). Each entry: c=syllable, l=level,
  e=example words. Add a meaning with "m":"..." or delete a syllable you do not want.
  "Rare / old" syllables (very low frequency) are hidden unless the box is ticked.

FONT: put DDC-Joyig.ttf in fonts/
